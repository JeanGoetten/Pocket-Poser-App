Pocket Poser — development privacy policy
Version: dev-2026-10-20-v8-local-engines-optional-remote

This personal app is under development. This document is a technical draft for testing, not a legally approved privacy policy for public distribution. A data controller, contact information, regions and contracts must be defined before public distribution.

The app lets you attach an existing video (the default option) or optionally record a video of one person using your device's camera app. Recording happens in the system camera app outside Pocket Poser's control: Pocket Poser does not access the camera directly, request camera or microphone permission, or request broad storage access. It only receives the file after recording finishes. Only use videos for which you have appropriate rights and permissions. Your consent does not automatically authorize processing other people's data.

After recording or selecting a video, a review screen lets you choose the segment and optionally resize a focus window around the intended person. Preparation starts only after you press Next: the video is copied to private storage, its audio is removed, and hashes are computed. On the device, it is converted to MP4/H.264 SDR at 30 frames per second with a maximum dimension of approximately 720 pixels. Source videos may be up to 250 MB for local normalization; the final normalized video sent to NVIDIA must not exceed 50 MB. HDR, unreadable videos, still images and videos longer than ten minutes are rejected. In ML Kit and MediaPipe modes, inference runs entirely on the device. One person is tracked in the normalized video. The experimental detector requires a visible full body and may fail or reject ambiguous tracking.

Each user creates their own NVIDIA Build account and enters an API key in My NVIDIA key. Default model: NVIDIA Body Pose (Nova-77). Fixed TLS destination: grpc.nvcf.nvidia.com:443. The Android flow does not use a Pocket Poser gateway or server. The key authenticates directly with NVIDIA and is not sent to another server. It is encrypted on the device with Android Keystore and excluded from backups and exports. Saving the key does not upload videos or verify authentication. Deleting or replacing the locally saved key does not revoke it at NVIDIA; you must revoke credentials through your NVIDIA account.

Sending is blocked until a key is saved and the NVIDIA account terms are confirmed. After configuring your key and granting authorization, only pressing Next on the review screen starts local preparation, tracking, and direct transmission. If you selected a focus window, only that cropped region of the prepared video, with padding when needed to preserve its proportions, is sent for inference. The sent interval excludes frames before the selected start and includes only frames before the selected end. The video without audio, tracking boxes and technical metadata are transmitted to obtain 3D poses. Confirm rights to the video, applicable terms and your account's free usage limits before submitting. The app does not arrange paid hosting but cannot control or guarantee the free availability of NVIDIA services. Failed or timed-out inference is never retried automatically.

Local data includes a versioned consent record with hash, scope, mandatory acknowledgement and optional remote authorization, plus a UTC timestamp; an encrypted personal key; temporary video files; skeletal data; and saved projects. Only NVIDIA is a Nova-77 provider. Local 33-point outputs are mapped to approximate 77-slot previews with uncalibrated rotations and must not be considered a Unity-ready rig. Original Nova-77 data and integrity metadata are retained. The preview T-pose, camera changes and corrections never replace original data. The offline demo is synthetic, uses no personal video, and is not an NVIDIA result. No analytics, advertising, marketing or model training are implemented. Logs must not contain keys, videos or poses.

To avoid repeating completed stages after failure, a pending processing job retains the audio-free source, normalized video and tracking boxes in private storage, outside backups and disposable cache. Completed progress can survive interruption, closing and reopening the app. The interface identifies the pending stage; resuming requires an explicit action and may resend the full video to NVIDIA. Reopening never triggers automatic upload. After inference, the unsaved result is kept as a durable private draft and restored on reopening without another upload. Saving, discarding or revoking deletes that draft; revocation preserves saved projects. Pending video files are removed after the result has been durably stored, choosing Discard processing, or revoking permission. Temporary files without a resume point are cleaned up on next startup. Saved projects remain on the device until explicitly deleted. Automatic backups and app transfer are disabled. Exported files are controlled by their selected destination. NVIDIA retention and remote deletion time frames cannot be guaranteed: check the provider's effective terms before uploading real videos.

You may decline, reread the policy or exit. Both boxes start unchecked; policy acknowledgement is required, but remote authorization is optional and may be granted separately. Revocation blocks new access and uploads, cancels local work and closes the NVIDIA connection. It cannot guarantee cancellation of a remote process already started, reimbursement, or remote deletion. No task data is stored in a gateway. Revocation does not delete saved projects or the key; there are separate actions to delete the key and local projects. Android settings allow clearing all local app data. Material changes to this document, version or purpose require renewed consent.

Before public distribution: review the data controller and contact, legal bases, rights-request channels, applicable regions, contractual retention, costs/quotas, terms/licenses and handling of children and teens. This prototype does not establish legal compliance.

Development v8 addendum: choose NVIDIA (remote), ML Kit or MediaPipe (offline) before selecting video. Offline processing does not send videos, landmarks or boxes to NVIDIA and needs no NVIDIA key. MediaPipe model assets are bundled. Local preview results are approximate with uncalibrated rotations. Aggregate local runtime, coverage and native-memory metrics remain in device-private storage. Offline jobs are not yet resumable after interruption.

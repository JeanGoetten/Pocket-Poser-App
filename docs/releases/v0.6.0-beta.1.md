## Scope

Import or record a video, select a segment and optional focus window, prepare frames locally, submit a selected single-person video for NVIDIA Body Pose inference using the user's own key and service entitlement, preview Nova-77 pose data, and save or export JSONL.

The application also offers synthetic demonstration data. Real-video inference can fail due to detection limitations, service availability, provider authorization or incompatible source video.

## Important limitations

This prerelease is intended for technical evaluation, **not production use**. It installs alongside regular builds using the `.debug` package suffix. Production-grade APK signing and legal review of the privacy policy and NVIDIA service terms remain pending. Do not upload video involving other people without the appropriate permissions.

Later private development improvements, including the 0.6.1/0.6.2 work, are **not** included in this source snapshot. Do not confuse the versions.
